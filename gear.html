Custom 3:1 Spur Gear Transmission System

Module: m = 2.5 mm Pinion Teeth: 18T Gear Teeth: 54T Pressure Angle: 20° Center Distance: 90 mm

Overview & Design Defense

Modeled a compact, high-precision 3:1 gear reduction drive (18T / 54T, Module 2.5) in SolidWorks. Tooth profiles were generated using fully parametric global equations, tying involute curves, pitch diameters, and root clearances directly to master design variables.


Summary Checklist for Design Review Defense:

  • Ratio Optimization: Achieves an exact target speed reduction ratio of 3:1 (54T / 18T).
  • Kinematic Reliability: Specifying 18 teeth on the driver pinion strictly avoids interference and profile undercutting without requiring complex negative profile shifts.
  • Compact Packaging: Saves 10 mm in center distance (90 mm vs 100 mm) and significantly reduces gearbox enclosure size compared to a conventional 20T/60T configuration.
  • Standardized Sourcing: Built upon a standard Module 2.5 involute profile, balancing maximum tooth bending strength with minimal cutter tooling costs.
