AutoCAD 2D Vastu Shastra Compliant Open Courtyard Front Open Garden

Designed tailored to exact site plot constraints (38'-5.75" x 40'-9"). This layout balances technical spatial requirements with Vastu Shastra principles and personalized lifestyle needs—combining an open courtyard and front garden with orthogonal living spaces across a non-parallel plot boundary.

Vastu Shastra & Personal Lifestyle Needs

Engineered to align directional energy flows with custom everyday usage requirements for a harmonious household layout.

  • Vastu Directional Zoning: Placed kitchen, bedrooms, and primary access points according to traditional Vastu guidelines.
  • Personalized Room Planning: Customized room dimensions to support family privacy, study areas, and client-specific living habits.
  • Brahmasthan Courtyard Integration: Maintained central openness via the internal courtyard to encourage positive energy circulation.

Open Courtyard & Front Garden Design

Combines outdoor greenery with an central open-to-sky courtyard to maximize natural ventilation and natural sunlight.

  • OPEN-1 Front Garden: Creates an inviting green entrance buffer while providing direct daylighting to front rooms.
  • Central Open Courtyard: Acts as an acoustic and natural ventilation hub, keeping interior rooms bright and well-ventilated.
  • OPEN-2 Utility Shaft: Dedicated 2'-6" x 2'-6" service shaft for discrete bath and plumbing exhaust ventilation.

Non-Standard Boundary Geometry

Accommodated an angled rear property line (38'-5.75") while maintaining strict 90° interior room angles for effortless utility.

  • Orthogonal Living Zones: Preserved 90-degree internal living areas for easy furniture placement and structural integrity.
  • Angular Variance Offset: Absorbed plot boundary angles into utility spaces and external offsets to keep key rooms square.
  • Wall Standard Specifications: Modeled 9-inch load-bearing perimeter brick walls and 4.5-inch internal partition walls.

Structural Column & CAD Discipline

Integrated reinforced concrete column markers directly into the 2D layout to guide structural engineering and vertical load paths.

  • Strategic Column Placement: Positioned 9" x 9" and 9" x 12" structural column blocks at load-bearing wall intersections.
  • Staircase Integration: Built structural framing around the dog-legged staircase adjacent to the car parking bay (7'-8.5" x 13'-0").
  • Layer Standardization: Standardized dynamic blocks, line weights, dimensions, and annotations for clear construction documentation.
